Essays are written in MDX: markdown, plus components dropped in where the argument
needs the reader's hands on something.

<Aside>Margin notes sit beside the text on wide screens and fall inline on phones.</Aside>

Here's the smallest version of that idea. Drag the control and the sentence answers back.
